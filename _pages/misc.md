In my spare time, I enjoy Chinese folk 🩰 and am a member of [Amber](https://www.cornellamberdance.com). You can [check out our dances here](https://space.bilibili.com/3493077077461127?spm_id_from=333.337.search-card.all.click).

I also value the balance and perspective that yoga and Buddhism bring to my daily life. 

When it comes to music, I take a lighter approach. Aware of the [10,000-hour rule](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/bjqsDJNRh25MNbpFK/the-10-000-hour-rule-is-a-myth), I don’t pursue mastery but rather play when the mood strikes. I rotate between piano, guitar, and erhu—each offering a different joy.
